# 🧪 AI Product Testing Portfolio

Manual QA work samples for **AI products** — especially Android voice assistants that speak English, Hindi and Hinglish.

By **Ritik Kumar** · AI Product Tester & QA at [The Hunter AI](https://github.com/HunterisLive-1)

> All examples here are written for practice on generic voice assistant scenarios. No private product code, internal bug reports or user data.

## 🔍 My Testing Approach

```
Reproduce  →  Document  →  Report  →  Retest
```

- **Real devices first** — budget, mid-range and flagship Android phones
- **Real conditions** — noisy rooms, weak network, low battery
- **Real language** — how people actually talk: Hindi, English and Hinglish mixed
